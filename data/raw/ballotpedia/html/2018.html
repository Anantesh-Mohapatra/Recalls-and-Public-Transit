Recalls are the process by which an elected official can be removed from office via a public vote. Click on the tabs below for more information about political recall efforts in 2018 by type of office. Click here to learn about how recall procedures vary from state to state.

In 2018, Ballotpedia tracked 206 recalls against 299 elected officials. Of the 123 elected officials who faced recall elections, 77 officials were recalled.


The following list may be incomplete. If you are aware of a recall effort not listed on this page, please contact editor@ballotpedia.org to let us know.
